# VYLLY

### Curated Interior Shopping & Content Platform

**Full-Stack Development · Payload CMS · Next.js · Content Systems**

[Live Website](https://vyllyapp.com/)

---

## Overview

VYLLY is a curated interior discovery and shopping platform I designed and developed.

The platform brings together interior styles, curated rooms, products, and editorial content.

Users can explore room ideas and discover products associated with each look.

VYLLY combines a custom content management system with a separate public-facing experience.

## The Product

The website organizes interior inspiration around connected content.

**Vibes → Rooms → Products**

Editorial content adds another way to discover curated interiors and selected items.

The platform is designed to connect inspiration with individual product discovery.

---

## 1. Content Management System

VYLLY uses a custom CMS built with Payload.

The CMS manages structured content through dedicated collections.

### Products

Product records support:

- Product information
- Images
- Product links
- Relationships with rooms
- Relationships with interior styles
- Related products

### Rooms

Room records connect curated interior concepts with selected products.

### Collections / Vibes

Interior styles organize rooms and product discovery into thematic collections.

### Journal

Editorial content is managed separately from product and room data.

### Media

The CMS includes media management for product and editorial imagery.

---

## 2. Data Relationships

VYLLY relies on structured relationships between collections.

The data model connects:

- Products
- Rooms
- Interior styles
- Related products
- Editorial content
- Media

This allows the frontend to present connected content without manually duplicating every relationship.

## 3. Frontend Development

The public-facing application is developed with Next.js.

The frontend includes:

- Homepage
- Interior style pages
- Room discovery
- Product detail pages
- Editorial content
- Product and room navigation
- Responsive layouts

## 4. Product Discovery

Products are presented as part of curated interior concepts.

Users can navigate from a style to a room and then to individual products.

External retailer links connect selected items to their product destinations.

## 5. Editorial Content

VYLLY includes journal content alongside structured product collections.

This supports interior guides, seasonal themes, and product discovery through editorial pages.

## 6. Smart Sync Integration

VYLLY also serves as a Payload CMS integration project for Smart Sync CMS.

The integration work includes:

- CMS connection workflows
- Collection discovery
- Document retrieval
- Structured content imports
- Field mapping
- Document create and update operations
- Media import workflows

This connects VYLLY's content management requirements with the broader Smart Sync platform.
